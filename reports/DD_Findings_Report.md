## 2. Headline findings

### Title is unproven (deal-blocking)
- The foundational 1998 Vermillion agreement is an **admin/co-publishing deal, not an assignment** — rights revert to writers on termination. Whether Northbridge ever owned (vs administered) the catalog is unresolved.
- The 2017 Vermillion→Northbridge deed in the room is **unexecuted** (blank signature blocks); the master purchase agreement and all schedules/annexes are missing.
- The masters chain rests on a 2014 Reston Vance/Capston assignment **not in the data room**.
- No writer agreements, no copyright registrations, anywhere.

### Revenue is overstated ~60–74% (price-defining)
- Seller's own detail documents reconcile to **~$470–512k/yr**, not $820k/$785k.
- The gap ≈ Capston's **label gross receipts (~$306k/yr)** counted as publishing revenue — Northbridge only collects an ~18% royalty on those.
- Summaries double-count a $42k BMW settlement (2022 *and* 2023), book a $30k PRS black-box payout never received, and include PRS/ASCAP lines 1.4–2.5× the actual publisher entitlement (writer-share double-counting).
- **Concentration is worse than disclosed:** December Rooftops ≈ **39%** of earnings (vs ~30% claimed), top 5 ≈ 65% (vs 56%) — and rising.

### Evidence of fabricated/forward-dated documents
- `Capston/2H-FY2026.pdf` covers Jan–Jun 2026 and is **issue-dated 28 Oct 2026 — in the future**.
- The "FINAL post call" term sheet is **byte-identical** to the draft and v1 (same MD5) — three filenames, one document.
- `scan_final.pdf` is actually Lena's holiday photo JPG renamed; 13 zero-byte placeholder files ("AWAITING_UPLOAD", lock files, failed exports).

## 5. Recommendation
