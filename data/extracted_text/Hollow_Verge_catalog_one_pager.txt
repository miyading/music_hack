                                       HOLLOW VERGE
                               Catalog Summary — for prospective buyer review


Overview
Northbridge Catalog Partners LLC is offering the Hollow Verge publishing catalog for sale. Hollow Verge was a
four-piece alt-rock band active 1996–2011, with four studio albums and several long-tail sync hits. Northbridge
acquired the catalog in November 2017 from Vermillion Music Publishing. The catalog has been administered by
Northbridge since acquisition.


What's included
• 100% worldwide publishing rights on the following albums:

 – Soft Static (1998) — 10 compositions

 – Pale Engines (2001) — 11 compositions

 – The Migrant Hour (2005) — 11 compositions

• Northbridge's interest in the master recordings of Pale Engines and The Migrant Hour

• All accrued income payable to Northbridge through the closing date

Catalog characteristics
• Long-tail sync earner. "December Rooftops" continues to attract recurring sync activity, including a recent
Netflix renewal.

• Stable performance income. Catalog earns steadily across PROs in 8+ territories.

• Modest US digital tailwind. Streaming growth on the masters of Pale Engines and The Migrant Hour continues
post-pandemic.

• Mature, declining baseline. Catalog is past commercial peak; conservatively budget low-single-digit annual
decline ex-sync.
