                             SUB-PUBLISHING AGREEMENT

               between Vermillion Music Publishing Ltd. and Editora Musical Atlântico Ltda.


Dated: 2009-01-15


Parties: (1) Vermillion Music Publishing Ltd. (the "Publisher"); (2) Editora Musical Atlântico Ltda. of Rua
Augusta 2914, conj. 81, São Paulo - SP, 01413-100, Brazil (the "Sub-Publisher").

1. APPOINTMENT AND TERRITORY
    1.1. The Publisher appoints the Sub-Publisher as its exclusive sub-publisher in the territory of The
    Federative Republic of Brazil (the "Territory") in respect of the Hollow Verge publishing catalog as it
    exists from time to time (the "Catalog").

2. COLLECTION AND RETENTION
    2.1. The Sub-Publisher shall collect, in the Territory, all royalties payable in respect of the Catalog,
    and shall remit to the Publisher the gross collected royalties less a retention of 20% (the
    "Sub-Publisher Retention").

3. ACCOUNTING
    3.1. The Sub-Publisher shall account to the Publisher in respect of each accounting period as
    specified in Schedule A, providing detailed statements no later than the period specified therein after
    the end of the relevant period. Remittance shall be in the local currency, with the option to convert to
    GBP or USD on Publisher's written request.

4. TERM
    4.1. This Agreement shall commence on the date hereof and shall continue for a term of 15 year(s),
    automatically renewing for successive 3 year terms unless terminated by either party on twelve (12)
    months' written notice prior to the end of the then-current term.

5. SCHEDULE A — ACCOUNTING PERIODS
    [See Schedule A attached. Standard cycles per territory: Italy semi-annual (calendar half-year); Brazil
    quarterly; Japan annual. Lag from period end to statement issue varies by territory.]

6. SPECIAL TERMS
    Remittance is made in Brazilian Real (BRL). The Sub-Publisher applies the Banco Central do Brasil
    reference rate of the last business day of the accounting period for any USD conversion requested by
    the Publisher.
