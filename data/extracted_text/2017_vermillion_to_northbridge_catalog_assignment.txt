                                   DEED OF ASSIGNMENT

                             Catalog Sale — Hollow Verge Publishing Catalog


Dated: 17 November 2017


Parties: (1) VERMILLION MUSIC PUBLISHING LTD., a private limited company registered in England
and Wales (company number 03114829), of 8 Hanover Square, London W1S 1JA (the "Assignor"); (2)
NORTHBRIDGE CATALOG PARTNERS LLC, a Delaware limited liability company of 1209 Orange
Street, Wilmington, DE 19801 (the "Assignee").

1. ASSIGNMENT
    1.1. The Assignor hereby assigns to the Assignee, with full title guarantee, all of its right, title, and
    interest in: (a) the musical compositions listed in Schedule 1 hereto (the "Catalog Compositions"); (b)
    the master recordings listed in Schedule 2 hereto (the "Catalog Masters"); and (c) the contracts,
    agreements, and accrued income listed in Schedule 3 hereto (collectively with (a) and (b), the
    "Catalog Assets").

2. CONSIDERATION
    2.1. In consideration of the assignment in clause 1, the Assignee shall pay the Assignor the sum
    stated in the master agreement of even date, payable in accordance with the schedule of payments
    therein.

3. SCHEDULES
    3.1. Schedule 1 (Catalog Compositions) — the compositions on the albums Soft Static (1998), Pale
    Engines (2001), and The Migrant Hour (2005), being forty (40) compositions in total. A full list of
    compositions, with ISWC numbers, is attached.

    3.2. Schedule 2 (Catalog Masters) — the master recordings of Pale Engines (2001) and The Migrant
    Hour (2005), comprising twenty-two (22) master recordings. The Assignor's interest in these masters
    was acquired from the administrator of Capston Records (Reston Vance Partners LLC) by private
    treaty dated 12 March 2014. A copy of the Reston Vance assignment is at Annex 4.

    3.3. Schedule 3 (Contracts and accrued income) — the sub-publishing agreements, sync licences,
    and accrued income described in Schedule 3, including without limitation Vermillion's interest in: the
    Cantata Edizioni Musicali sub-publishing agreement (Italy, 2010); the Editora Musical Atlântico
    sub-publishing agreement (Brazil, 2009); and the Pulse Music Group sync agency appointment
    (2014).

    3.4. Excluded from this assignment: (a) the master recordings of Soft Static (1998), which remain
    with the Assignor's residuary; (b) any rights in Ordinary Weather (2010), which were never owned by
    the Assignor; (c) any rights in the Japanese B-sides EP "■■■ / Night Sounds" (2003), the
    sub-publishing of which reverts to Asahi Editions K.K. on 1 September 2018 under the original
    sub-publishing agreement; and (d) any rights or income in respect of the solo recording work of any
   individual writer (specifically including but not limited to Mara Solano's solo recordings administered by
   Stonecut Music Publishing).

4. ACCRUED LIABILITIES
   4.1. The Assignee acknowledges and accepts the accrued liabilities set out in Schedule 4 hereto,
   including without limitation any ongoing obligations under the sample-clearance side letter dated 12
   April 2001 between Vermillion and Bug Music in respect of "Slow Engine."

5. GOVERNING LAW
   5.1. This Deed shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of New York. The
   parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the State of New York and the United States
   Federal courts located therein.
