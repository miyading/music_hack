            CO-PUBLISHING AND ADMINISTRATION AGREEMENT

                   between Hollow Verge Partnership and Vermillion Music Publishing


Dated: 20 September 1998


Parties: (1) The four members of the Hollow Verge partnership (collectively, the "Writers"); (2) Vermillion
Music Publishing Ltd. of 8 Hanover Square, London W1S 1JA ("Vermillion").

1. GRANT OF RIGHTS
    1.1. The Writers hereby grant to Vermillion, for the term of this Agreement, the exclusive worldwide
    right to administer all musical compositions written by the Writers during the term of this Agreement
    and the previous twelve months (the "Compositions"), including without limitation the right to collect all
    royalties payable in respect of the Compositions.

    1.2. Vermillion shall be entitled to the publisher's share of all royalties in respect of the Compositions,
    in accordance with standard practice in the territories concerned. The Writers retain the writer's share
    in full, subject to clause 2.

2. ADMINISTRATION FEE
    2.1. Vermillion shall retain an administration fee of fifteen per cent (15%) of the writer's share of all
    royalties collected on behalf of the Writers. The remaining eighty-five per cent (85%) shall be remitted
    to the Writers in accordance with clause 4.

3. TERM
    3.1. This Agreement shall commence on the date hereof and shall continue for an initial term of five
    (5) years, automatically renewing for successive three (3) year terms unless terminated by either party
    on twelve (12) months' written notice prior to the end of the then-current term.

    3.2. On termination, the rights granted to Vermillion shall continue for a wind-down period of two (2)
    years in respect of Compositions written during the term, in order to permit the orderly collection of
    royalties already accrued. Thereafter, all rights shall revert to the Writers or such party as they may
    appoint.

4. ACCOUNTING AND PAYMENT
    4.1. Vermillion shall account to the Writers in respect of each calendar half-year, no later than ninety
    (90) days after the end of the relevant period, providing a detailed statement of all royalties collected,
    the administration fee retained, and the net amount payable.

    4.2. The Writers may, on reasonable notice, audit Vermillion's books and records in respect of any
    accounting period within twelve (12) months of receipt of the statement for that period.

5. SUB-PUBLISHING
    5.1. Vermillion may appoint sub-publishers in any territory outside the United Kingdom on customary
    terms, provided that the aggregate sub-publishing fee in any territory shall not exceed twenty-five per
    cent (25%) of the gross collected royalties in that territory without the Writers' written consent.



This Agreement is signed by the parties on the date first written above.
