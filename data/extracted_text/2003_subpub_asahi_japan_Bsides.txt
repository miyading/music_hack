                             SUB-PUBLISHING AGREEMENT

                    between Vermillion Music Publishing Ltd. and Asahi Editions K.K.


Dated: 2003-01-15


Parties: (1) Vermillion Music Publishing Ltd. (the "Publisher"); (2) Asahi Editions K.K. of 2-14-1 Akasaka,
Minato-ku, Tokyo 107-0052, Japan (the "Sub-Publisher").

1. APPOINTMENT AND TERRITORY
    1.1. The Publisher appoints the Sub-Publisher as its exclusive sub-publisher in the territory of Japan
    (the "Territory") in respect of the Hollow Verge publishing catalog as it exists from time to time (the
    "Catalog").

2. COLLECTION AND RETENTION
    2.1. The Sub-Publisher shall collect, in the Territory, all royalties payable in respect of the Catalog,
    and shall remit to the Publisher the gross collected royalties less a retention of 40% (the
    "Sub-Publisher Retention").

3. ACCOUNTING
    3.1. The Sub-Publisher shall account to the Publisher in respect of each accounting period as
    specified in Schedule A, providing detailed statements no later than the period specified therein after
    the end of the relevant period. Remittance shall be in the local currency, with the option to convert to
    GBP or USD on Publisher's written request.

4. TERM
    4.1. This Agreement shall commence on the date hereof and shall continue for a term of 15 year(s),
    automatically renewing for successive 3 year terms unless terminated by either party on twelve (12)
    months' written notice prior to the end of the then-current term.

    4.2. Notwithstanding clause 4.1, on the 15th anniversary of this Agreement, all rights granted to the
    Publisher under any prior sub-publishing arrangement covering the same catalog within the Territory
    shall revert to the Sub-Publisher, provided that any income accrued but not yet collected shall
    continue to be remitted to the Publisher under the terms of clause 2.

5. SCHEDULE A — ACCOUNTING PERIODS
    [See Schedule A attached. Standard cycles per territory: Italy semi-annual (calendar half-year); Brazil
    quarterly; Japan annual. Lag from period end to statement issue varies by territory.]

6. SPECIAL TERMS
    This Agreement covers ONLY the Japanese B-sides EP "■■■ / Night Sounds" (2003), and
    specifically does NOT cover any other Hollow Verge catalog title. On the 15th anniversary of the EP's
  commercial release (1 September 2018), all sub-publishing rights in the B-sides EP revert to the
  Sub-Publisher in perpetuity. The Publisher retains no continuing interest in the B-sides EP from that
  date.
