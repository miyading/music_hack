                              PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

                                           HOLLOW VERGE


THIS PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT is made and entered into as of the 14th day of September, 1998, by
and between MARA IOLANDA SOLANO, DEVENDRA ASHFORD, JULIAN PETROV, and COEN
THEODORE WHITLOCK (each a "Partner" and collectively the "Partners"), each domiciled at the
addresses set out in Schedule A hereto.

1. FORMATION AND NAME
   1.1. The Partners hereby form a partnership (the "Partnership") under the laws of England and Wales,
   operating under the artistic name "Hollow Verge" (the "Name").

   1.2. The principal place of business of the Partnership shall be such address as the Partners may
   from time to time agree in writing.

2. PURPOSE
   2.1. The purpose of the Partnership is to engage in the business of: (a) the creation, recording, and
   exploitation of musical compositions and sound recordings; (b) the live performance of music; (c) the
   merchandising of goods associated with the Name; and (d) such other artistic and commercial
   activities as the Partners may from time to time agree.

3. PARTNERSHIP SHARES AND BAND-WRITTEN COMPOSITIONS
   3.1. The Partners shall share equally — that is, in equal shares of twenty-five per cent (25%) each —
   in all profits, losses, assets, and liabilities of the Partnership.

   3.2. For any musical composition created jointly by two (2) or more Partners under the Name of the
   Partnership (a "Band-Written Composition"), the writer's share and the publisher's share of all royalties
   and other income shall be allocated twenty-five per cent (25%) to each Partner, irrespective of
   which Partner(s) made the primary creative contribution.

   3.3. The Partners acknowledge that, from time to time, third-party writers may contribute to a
   Band-Written Composition. Where such contribution is documented in a written split sheet signed by
   all contributing writers, the third-party writer's share shall be deducted from the aggregate Partner
   share and the remaining Partner share shall be redistributed equally amongst the four Partners.

   3.4. The Partners further acknowledge that any composition created by a Partner outside the scope of
   the Partnership (a "Solo Composition") is not subject to this Agreement and remains the sole property
   of the Partner who created it.

4. MASTER RECORDINGS
   4.1. All master recordings of Band-Written Compositions created during the term of any recording
   agreement entered into by the Partnership shall be governed by that recording agreement.
    4.2. Any master recording created by the Partnership outside the scope of an active recording
    agreement (an "Independent Master") shall be owned equally by the four Partners as tenants in
    common, with each Partner holding an undivided twenty-five per cent (25%) interest.

5. SCHEDULE OF COMPOSITIONS
    5.1. Annexed to this Agreement as Schedule B is the initial list of Band-Written Compositions existing
    as of the date hereof, which the Partners acknowledge are governed by this Agreement.

    5.2. The Partners shall update Schedule B from time to time to reflect Band-Written Compositions
    created after the date of this Agreement. Failure to update Schedule B shall not affect the treatment of
    any Band-Written Composition under clause 3.

6. DECISIONS, MANAGEMENT, AND DISPUTES
    6.1. All material decisions of the Partnership shall require the affirmative vote of a majority (three of
    four) Partners. Material decisions include but are not limited to: entering into recording agreements,
    publishing agreements, and sync licences in excess of £10,000; admission of a new Partner; and
    dissolution of the Partnership.

    6.2. Day-to-day administrative decisions may be taken by any Partner acting alone, provided that such
    decisions are reported to the other Partners in writing within a reasonable period.

7. WITHDRAWAL, DEATH, AND ADMISSION OF NEW PARTNERS
    7.1. Upon the death of a Partner, that Partner's share in the Partnership (including the writer's and
    publisher's share of all Band-Written Compositions written before the date of death) shall vest in the
    estate of the deceased Partner.

    7.2. A Partner may voluntarily withdraw from the Partnership upon ninety (90) days' written notice to
    the remaining Partners. Such withdrawal does not extinguish the withdrawing Partner's share in works
    created during the period of their participation in the Partnership.

8. TERM AND TERMINATION
    8.1. This Agreement shall continue until terminated by the unanimous written agreement of the
    Partners.

    8.2. Upon termination, the Partnership shall be wound up in accordance with the laws of England and
    Wales. All royalty and other rights in Band-Written Compositions shall continue to be allocated 25% to
    each Partner (or their estate) in perpetuity.

9. GOVERNING LAW
    9.1. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and
    Wales. The Partners submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
