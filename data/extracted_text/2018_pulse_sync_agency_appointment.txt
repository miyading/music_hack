                    SYNC LICENSING AGENCY APPOINTMENT

                between Northbridge Catalog Partners LLC and Pulse Music Group, LLC


Dated: 8 January 2018


Northbridge Catalog Partners LLC (the "Catalog Owner") hereby appoints Pulse Music Group, LLC (the
"Agent") as its exclusive non-master synchronisation licensing agent in respect of the Hollow Verge
publishing catalog. The Agent shall procure synchronisation licences, negotiate fees, and remit the gross
fee to the Catalog Owner less an agent fee of fifteen per cent (15%). All licences shall be subject to the
Catalog Owner's approval. The Agent shall provide quarterly summary statements of all placements.

Term: three (3) years from the date hereof, renewable by mutual agreement. Either party may terminate on
six (6) months' written notice in respect of new placements; existing placements survive termination.
